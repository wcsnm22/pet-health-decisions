Embark review, cost, quote and discount — FAQ

How much does an Embark dog DNA test cost?

The Breed + Health test is listed at $139, while the Breed ID Dog DNA Test and the Dog Age Test are listed at $109 each, as shown on Embark's product pages on 2026-09-25. Promotions change the displayed price.

Source: https://embarkvet.com/products/breed-and-health-dog-dna-test-kit · checked 2026-09-25

How long do Embark results take?

Results come back in 2-4 weeks according to the product instructions, and Embark's FAQ adds that they are typically ready within a few weeks of your sample arriving at the lab - you are notified by email.

Source: https://embarkvet.com/products/breed-and-health-dog-dna-test-kit · checked 2026-09-25

Is a DNA test worth it if my dog seems healthy?

Embark's FAQ answers that genetic health risks do not always show symptoms right away, so testing early means you and your vet can watch for a risk before it becomes a bigger problem.

Source: https://embarkvet.com/products/breed-and-health-dog-dna-test-kit · checked 2026-09-25

What happens if my Embark results show a health risk?

Embark says you get a clear explanation of what the result means along with next steps to bring to your vet, and notes that genetic testing is an assessment of risk, not a diagnosis.

Source: https://embarkvet.com/products/breed-and-health-dog-dna-test-kit · checked 2026-09-25

How is Embark different from other dog DNA tests?

Embark says it is a research partner of Cornell University College of Veterinary Medicine with one of the world's largest dog DNA databases, and that Breed + Health adds 270+ genetic health condition screening, traits and Allergy Risk Scores on top of breed mix and ancestry.

Source: https://embarkvet.com/products/breed-and-health-dog-dna-test-kit · checked 2026-09-25
