Vetster review, cost, quote and discount — FAQ

How much does Vetster cost?

Vetster Plus is $12/month, billed annually, and includes 4 video appointments a year plus unlimited messaging with a certified veterinary professional. If you pay per appointment instead, the single appointment is billed at marketplace rates and the Vetster Plus page prints it as 'Starting at $102 one-time'.

Source: https://vetster.com/en-us/plus · checked 2026-09-25

Is Vetster available in my state?

Vetster states that both Vetster Plus and per-appointment payment options are available in all states, and that appointments are with licensed veterinarians.

Source: https://vetster.com/en-us/plus · checked 2026-09-25

Can an online vet on Vetster prescribe medication?

Vetster states its veterinarians can prescribe online: the vet may decide your pet needs a prescription during the appointment, you add the medication to your cart in the app, and you order it through VetsterRx or pick it up from your local pharmacy. Prescriptions are available in select states.

Source: https://vetster.com/en-us/vetster-rx-online-pharmacy · checked 2026-09-25

How much are VetsterRx pharmacy and shipping fees?

Pharmacy fee per order is $7 for Vetster Plus members and $14 for non-members; the compounding fee is $10 for members and $20 for non-members. Standard shipping is free for members and for non-member orders over $45, while express shipping is $19.

Source: https://vetster.com/en-us/vetster-rx-online-pharmacy · checked 2026-09-25

Can I cancel Vetster Plus?

Vetster's FAQ says you may cancel your subscription at any time, but the membership must be canceled before it renews to avoid the next billing cycle.

Source: https://vetster.com/en-us/plus · checked 2026-09-25
