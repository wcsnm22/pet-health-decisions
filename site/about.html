About

Pet Health Decisions is a small static site that answers pet health questions - online vets, pet pharmacies, supplements and DNA tests - with facts taken only from each brand's own official website and official help or product pages.

How it works:

  • Every fact on this site carries the official source page URL it was read from and the date it was checked.
  • Nothing is estimated, rounded, or filled in from memory. If a figure is not published on the brand's official site, it is not repeated here.
  • Promotions are reported only when the brand's own site publishes them. Where no official promo page exists, the page says so.
  • We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or compensated by any brand listed on this site. Product labels, terms and your veterinarian's advice always govern.

The site is open source: https://github.com/wcsnm22/pet-health-decisions. Facts were last checked 2026-09-25.
