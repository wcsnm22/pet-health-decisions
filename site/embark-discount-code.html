Do Embark codes from coupon sites work?

Embark's own troubleshooting article says they often do not: codes found on external coupon sites may not be honored because they can be incorrect and may not represent Embark's current offers. It also lists three reasons a valid-looking code fails - it is specific to certain kits or order totals, or it has expired.

Is there a discount for first-time buyers?

Embark's help center says it is not currently running a specific first-time purchaser sale and points to its current sale prices instead. The closest official equivalent is Refer A Friend, where a first-time customer gets $10 off a Breed + Health or Purebred kit priced at $139 or more.

Source: https://help.embarkvet.com/hc/en-us/articles/56238423425819-Do-you-have-a-sale-for-first-time-purchasers · checked 2026-09-26

How does Refer A Friend work?

You enter your name and email on Embark's referral page, then share your unique link or code. Your friend must be a first-time customer buying a Breed + Health or Purebred kit priced at $139 or more; after their purchase you get a $10 coupon by email, it expires 90 days after it is sent, and referral discounts cannot be combined with other offers.

Source: https://help.embarkvet.com/hc/en-us/articles/56240110599195-How-does-Embark-s-Refer-A-Friend-program-work · checked 2026-09-26

Can I stack two discounts?

Not with the referral program - Embark prints that referral discounts cannot be combined with other offers, and the veterinary professional discount says the same. For promo codes generally, Embark says some codes work only on specific kit types or orders over a certain total, so check the email or page where the offer came from.

Source: https://help.embarkvet.com/hc/en-us/articles/56240110599195-How-does-Embark-s-Refer-A-Friend-program-work · checked 2026-09-26

Are there vet, breeder or shelter discounts?

Yes, all published in the help center: veterinary professionals get a one-time $10 off a Breed + Health or Purebred kit with proof of role; buyers of multiple kits are pointed to Bundle & Save, tiered breeder kit pricing, litter packages, and a shelter and rescue program.
