How to buy pet prescription meds online legally: the vet authorization step comes first

Quick answer: you cannot buy a prescription pet medication online legally without your veterinarian's authorization, and both pharmacies say so in their own words. Chewy Pharmacy states it can only dispense medication with a valid, patient-specific prescription from a licensed veterinarian; 1-800-PetMeds states that all prescription medications must have an authorization before they are dispensed. The buying itself is three steps: add the medication to the cart, enter your pet's and your vet clinic's details at checkout, and choose how the pharmacy gets the script - they contact your vet, or you mail a written one in. Then the clock: Chewy prints prescription processing within 24-48 hours after the pharmacy receives a valid script and delivery in 3-5 days once it is approved; 1-800-PetMeds prints vet approval required before shipment and standard delivery in 2-5 business days. The three steps, then the clock

The order itself is short; the authorization is the part that makes it legal. Chewy publishes the sequence as three steps: add your pet's meds with a click, enter a few quick details at checkout (your pet's information and your vet clinic's details so the pharmacy can verify the prescription with your vet), and let the pharmacy do the rest - its licensed pharmacy team reviews the prescription medications in the order to make sure they are safe and accurate for your pet.

Then two clocks run. Chewy prints a 24-48 hour processing window after the pharmacy receives a valid prescription and a 3-5 day delivery window after approval. 1-800-PetMeds prints the approval gate first - vet approval is required before shipment - and then standard delivery in 2-5 business days, with free shipping on orders over $49.

The four things people get wrong

Approval is not shipping. 1-800-PetMeds puts vet approval before shipment in the same sentence as its delivery window, so a fast shipping estimate means nothing until your vet signs off. Chewy separates them too: processing time starts when the valid prescription reaches the pharmacy, delivery time starts when it is approved.

Mail-in means the original. Chewy states plainly that it requires the original paper prescription, not a copy, and that your order number must be on it to avoid delays - and Gabapentin orders go to a different address in Pittston, PA. 1-800-PetMeds asks for your phone number or order number on anything mailed to its Delray Beach pharmacy.

Compounded is not the same as manufactured. Chewy defines compounding as licensed pharmacists mixing or changing one or more FDA-approved medications, and it states its compounding pharmacy is licensed in Kentucky and in each state it ships to, with PCAB accreditation.

Authorization is recorded. 1-800-PetMeds stores written and faxed prescriptions electronically and records phone authorizations, which is why the vet can call the pharmacy line directly instead of sending you paperwork.

Buying pet prescription meds online FAQ

Is it legal to buy pet medication online?

Yes, when the pharmacy dispenses against a valid prescription from a licensed veterinarian - that is the legal line both pharmacies describe. Chewy Pharmacy states it can only dispense medication with a valid, patient-specific prescription from a licensed veterinarian, and its compounding pharmacy states it is licensed in Kentucky and in every state it ships compounded medication to. 1-800-PetMeds states all prescription medications must have an authorization before they are dispensed.

Source: https://www.chewy.com/sildenafil-compounded-capsule-dogs/dp/316936 · checked 2026-09-26

Do I need the prescription in hand before I order?

At Chewy, no. Its FAQ says a written prescription is not required when you place the order; you pick at checkout whether Chewy contacts your vet or you mail the script, and the prescription has to be on file before dispensing. 1-800-PetMeds does not publish that ordering detail on the pages checked - it publishes that vet approval is required before shipment, so we leave that cell empty rather than guessing.

Source: https://www.chewy.com/sildenafil-compounded-capsule-dogs/dp/316936 · checked 2026-09-26

How long does the whole order take?

Chewy prints two windows: prescriptions are typically processed within 24-48 hours after the pharmacy receives a valid prescription, and delivery takes 3-5 days once the prescription is approved (a mailed paper prescription typically ships within 1-2 days after receipt). 1-800-PetMeds prints standard delivery in 2-5 business days with vet approval required before shipment.

Source: https://www.chewy.com/sildenafil-compounded-capsule-dogs/dp/316936 · checked 2026-09-26

What if my vet gives me a paper prescription?

At Chewy, place the order and choose mail-in prescription at checkout, then send the original - not a copy - with your order number to Chewy Pharmacy, 3621 Fern Valley Rd., Louisville, KY 40219 (Gabapentin orders: 360 Research Drive, Pittston, PA 18640); Chewy covers overnight shipping for it. At 1-800-PetMeds, mail it to Attn: Pharmacy, 420 S Congress Ave. #100, Delray Beach, FL 33445 with your phone or order number, or have your vet call 1-888-738-6331.

Source: https://www.chewy.com/app/content/pharmacy · checked 2026-09-26

Are these the same drugs my vet sells?

Chewy says it sells the same FDA-approved meds as veterinary clinics and that its licensed pharmacy team checks each medication in the order. 1-800-PetMeds says it sells only U.S. FDA/EPA-approved medications - the same exact medications sold by your veterinarian - and that its products and facilities are regularly inspected by state agencies.

Source: https://www.chewy.com/app/content/pharmacy · checked 2026-09-26

Can I set up refills for a chronic condition?

Chewy says that with valid refills on file at Chewy Pharmacy you can set up Autoship for the medication. 1-800-PetMeds' help page describes adding a prescription item to AutoShip: the pharmacy reaches out to your veterinarian for approval before shipping, and that request does not delay the existing AutoShip order.

Source: https://www.chewy.com/sildenafil-compounded-capsule-dogs/dp/316936 · checked 2026-09-26

What does compounded medication mean?

Chewy defines compounding as licensed pharmacists mixing or changing one or more FDA-approved medications to make a customized medication for a pet - for example a liquid instead of a tablet, or a strength that is not commercially available. Chewy states that all compounding ingredients are sourced from FDA registered manufacturers and distributors.

Source: https://www.chewy.com/sildenafil-compounded-capsule-dogs/dp/316936 · checked 2026-09-26
