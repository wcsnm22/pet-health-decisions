FAQ

Can a veterinarian see my pet online?

Yes - Vetster lets you book video appointments with licensed veterinarians 24/7 and says both its subscription and per-appointment options are available in all states. Both options include secure video calls, 48-hour follow-up live messaging with your vet, and access to thousands of veterinarians.

Source: https://vetster.com/en-us/plus · checked 2026-09-25

How do online pet pharmacies handle prescription medication?

1-800-PetMeds states that for prescription items a veterinarian's approval is required before shipment and that it reaches out to your veterinarian for approval before shipping. Vetster works differently: its vet decides during your appointment whether a prescription is needed, and you order it through VetsterRx or pick it up at a local pharmacy.

Source: https://www.1800petmeds.com/ · checked 2026-09-25

How much does shipping cost when I order pet medication online?

On the brands' own published figures: 1-800-PetMeds gives free shipping on orders over $49 with standard delivery in 2-5 business days, and VetsterRx standard shipping is free for Vetster Plus members and for non-member orders over $45, with express shipping at $19.
