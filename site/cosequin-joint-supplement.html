Cosequin review, cost, quote and discount — FAQ

What is Cosequin?

Cosequin is a joint supplement line from Nutramax for dogs, cats and horses. Cosequin's site says the #1 veterinarian recommended retail joint health supplement contains ingredients backed by scientific research and is available in a variety of formulas to support joint health.

Source: https://www.cosequin.com/ · checked 2026-09-25

Which pets can take Cosequin?

Dogs, cats and horses - Cosequin publishes separate product lines for each, with formats that include soft chews, chewable tablets, sprinkle capsules, tidbits, pellets, powder and tablets.

Source: https://www.cosequin.com/ · checked 2026-09-25

Do veterinarians recommend Cosequin?

Cosequin states it is the #1 veterinarian recommended retail joint health supplement brand, based on a survey conducted among small animal veterinarians who recommended oral joint health supplements, counting retail brands only.

Source: https://www.cosequin.com/ · checked 2026-09-25

How much does Cosequin cost?

Cosequin's official site does not publish prices - it lists the products and points shoppers to a Where to Buy option, so the price is set by the retailer. Compare the per-serving size of the formula you pick when you check retailers.

Source: https://www.cosequin.com/ · checked 2026-09-25
