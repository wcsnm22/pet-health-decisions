Vetster review: what Vetster's own pages say about price, appointments and prescriptions

Quick answer: Vetster is a marketplace where you book a licensed veterinarian for a video appointment, 24/7. On its own published numbers there are two ways to pay - Vetster Plus at $12/month billed annually, which includes 4 same-day video appointments a year, or a single appointment starting at $102 one-time. Both options include 48-hour follow-up live messaging with your vet and prescriptions through VetsterRx. Prescribing is always the veterinarian's decision and depends on your state's rules, and Vetster states online vets are not a replacement for emergency clinics. What the numbers say side by side

The subscription and the single visit are priced on different units, so compare the units first. Vetster prints $12/month and then states in its own FAQ that billing occurs annually, once per year. A single appointment is printed as a one-time starting price of $102 and billed at whatever the chosen veterinarian's marketplace rate is - that is why the table says starting at and not costs.

The fee table is where the two options quietly diverge. The same pharmacy page prints both rates side by side: $7 vs $14 per order, $10 vs $20 per compounding, free standard shipping vs free only above $45. Express shipping is $19 either way.

Where an online vet stops

Vetster is explicit about the boundary, and a review that hides it would be useless. Its own guide says to seek immediate in-person or emergency care for seven signs: difficulty breathing, uncontrolled bleeding, collapse or seizures, severe or sudden pain, suspected poisoning, major trauma, and being unable to urinate. Its preparation guide repeats the point in different words - telehealth is not a substitute for emergency care.

Prescribing sits in the middle. Vetster's prescription guide says a valid vet-client-patient relationship is required in almost every state, that the open question is whether that relationship can be built over video, that federal guidance often expects a real exam first, and that prescribing is always the veterinarian's decision. Practical consequence for a reviewer: the appointment can end with "your pet needs to be seen in person", and that is the clinic standard, not a runaround.

Vetster review FAQ

How much does Vetster cost?

Vetster Plus is $12/month, billed annually. Twelve months at that published price comes to $144 (12 x $12, arithmetic on Vetster's own figure) and it includes 4 same-day video appointments a year plus unlimited messaging with registered vet techs. A single appointment starts at $102 one-time at marketplace rates. Products, pharmacy fees and shipping are charged separately.

Source: https://vetster.com/en-us/plus · checked 2026-09-26

What is the difference between Vetster Plus and paying per appointment?

Vetster Plus is a yearly subscription: 4 same-day video appointments a year, 50% off additional bookings, up to 2 unused appointments carried over, unlimited messaging with registered vet techs, all pets under one plan. Paying per appointment buys one visit for one pet at marketplace rates, starting at $102 one-time, with the 48-hour follow-up messaging included.

Source: https://vetster.com/en-us/plus · checked 2026-09-26

Can a Vetster vet prescribe medication?

Yes, when a valid vet-client-patient relationship is in place and the condition can be handled safely over video. Whether that relationship can be established online depends on your state's rules, federal guidance often expects a real exam first, and prescribing is always the veterinarian's decision. The prescription is then sent to the pharmacy you choose - Vetster is not a pharmacy.

Source: https://vetster.com/en/wellness/online-vet-prescriptions · checked 2026-09-26

What are Vetster's pharmacy and shipping fees?

Pharmacy fee per order: $7 for Vetster Plus members, $14 for non-members. Compounding fee per medication: $10 for members, $20 for non-members. Standard shipping (2-3 business days) is free for members and for non-member orders over $45; express shipping (1-2 business days) is $19 for everyone.

Source: https://vetster.com/en-us/vetster-rx-online-pharmacy · checked 2026-09-26

Is Vetster available in my state?

Both payment options are listed as available in all states, and VetsterRx fulfillment is listed as available in all 50 States. Whether a vet can prescribe during an online visit still depends on your state's rules, so that is confirmed during the appointment itself.

Source: https://vetster.com/en-us/plus · checked 2026-09-26

When should I not use an online vet?

Go in person immediately for difficulty breathing, uncontrolled bleeding, collapse or seizures, severe or sudden pain, suspected poisoning, major trauma, or being unable to urinate. Vetster states online vets are not a replacement for emergency clinics and that an online vet will direct you to appropriate in-person care.

Source: https://vetster.com/en/wellness/online-vet-visits-how-virtual-veterinary-care-works · checked 2026-09-26

How long can I message my vet after the appointment?

48-hour follow-up live messaging with your vet is included in both payment options. Vetster Plus separately includes unlimited messaging with registered vet techs for help with pet health, behaviour, nutrition and urgent advice.

Source: https://vetster.com/en-us/plus · checked 2026-09-26
