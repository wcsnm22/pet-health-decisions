1-800-PetMeds review, cost, quote and discount — FAQ

Do I need a prescription to order medication from 1-800-PetMeds?

For prescription items, yes: the site states that a veterinarian's approval is required before shipment and that it reaches out to your veterinarian for prescription approval before shipping.

Source: https://www.1800petmeds.com/ · checked 2026-09-25

How fast is 1-800-PetMeds shipping and how much does it cost?

Standard delivery is 2-5 business days and shipping is free on orders over $49, according to the shipping banner on the site.

Source: https://www.1800petmeds.com/ · checked 2026-09-25

What is AutoShip?

AutoShip is the recurring order program: you lock in 5% off future orders, get an email one week before each order ships, can skip or pause online, and can schedule a free video call with a licensed vet while enrolled.

Source: https://www.1800petmeds.com/ · checked 2026-09-25

Does 1-800-PetMeds price match?

Yes - the site says 'See a lower price somewhere? We'll match it!' and asks you to contact them so they can verify the lower price, backed by a 100% satisfaction guarantee.

Source: https://www.1800petmeds.com/ · checked 2026-09-25

Is there a discount code on the official site right now?

On 2026-09-25 the site displayed 20% off select supplements with code SUP20, with savings of up to $20 per order. Promotion terms apply and offers change, so check the banner on 1800petmeds.com before ordering.

Source: https://www.1800petmeds.com/ · checked 2026-09-25
